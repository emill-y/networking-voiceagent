Ten seconds after a conversation.

1

Say it

Tap the orb and talk like you’d tell a friend. Your iPhone turns it into text as you speak.

2

Check it

Nomi pulls out names, companies, what you talked about and what you promised. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

3

Remember it

Every person gets a memory page and a timeline, so each new conversation adds to the last one instead of replacing it.

Before you meet again

Open anyone’s page for a short briefing built only from your own notes: when you last met, what they were working on, and what you said you’d do.

You last saw Jason 6 weeks ago at Cal Hacks.· You planned to: introduce him to Maya.· Ask how the robotics startup is going.

Ask in plain words

“Who did I meet at the career fair?” “Who works at Figma?” Nomi searches your memories and tells you why each person matched.

See your network

An interactive map of how the people you know connect: same company or school, same event, shared interests.

Keep your promises

“I’ll send the deck Friday” becomes a follow-up with a due date. Tick it off when it’s done.

Start with your contacts

Bring in the people you already know. Matching happens on your phone, and only the people you pick are added.

For students

Recruiting season is a people game.

Career fairs, club events, office hours, the person next to you in lecture. Nomi knows your school, so “my TA” or “a classmate” lands in the right place. You’ll walk into every coffee chat knowing exactly what you talked about last time, and you’ll actually send the resume you promised.

  • “Who did I meet at the career fair?”
  • “Who’s interning at companies I’m applying to?”
  • “Who offered to refer me?”

Private by design.

Only when you tap

Nomi never listens in the background, and it shows clearly when it’s listening.

Voice stays on your phone

Speech becomes text on your iPhone whenever the device supports it. Recordings aren’t stored.

You’re in control

Edit or delete anything. Export everything. Delete your account and all of it goes with it.

Never sold

No ads, no tracking, no looking people up behind your back, and no messages sent for you.
