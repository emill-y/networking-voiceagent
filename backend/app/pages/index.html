How a session works

One tap at the door. Everyone, remembered.

1

Start a session

Name it (“Cal Hacks mixer”) or don’t. One tap and it’s listening, even with the screen locked.

2

Just talk

Nomi picks out each person you meet as they introduce themselves, and fills in what they do and what you talked about as the night goes on.

3

Review in a minute

End the session and see everyone in one list. Fix a detail, uncheck anyone you don’t want, and save. The audio is never kept.

Smart search

Ask about anyone, in plain words.

Nomi searches by meaning, across every session. “Who could help me with chemistry?” finds the chemical engineer, even if neither of you said the word.

  • “Who did I meet at Cal Hacks?”
  • “Who’s looking for a designer?”
  • “That founder from last week who does food”

Private by design

Notes, not recordings.

Audio is never stored

Sound is turned into text in 20-second pieces and thrown away. Only the details you confirm are saved.

Nothing saves without you

A session finds people; you decide who to keep at the end. Discard a session and it’s gone.

Only you can see it

Your network is yours: never sold, never used for ads, and you can export or delete it any time.

Questions

Is it okay to record at an event?

Let people know you’re taking notes. Some places, including California, require everyone in a conversation to agree to be recorded, and Nomi asks you to confirm that before your first session. Nomi only keeps the notes, never the audio.

Does my phone need to stay out?

No. Start the session and put your phone in your pocket. It keeps listening with the screen locked, and you can check who it has found any time.

What if it gets someone wrong?

Nothing is saved until you review the session. You can fix any detail, add someone it missed, or leave someone out.

Does it work for long events?

Yes, up to four hours per session. If your connection drops, Nomi keeps recording and catches up when it’s back.

Where can I use it?

On iPhone and the web, in early access. Join the waitlist and founding members get in first.
