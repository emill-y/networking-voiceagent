FAQ

What does it cost?

Nomi is free while it’s in early access. If we ever add paid plans, early users will hear first, and your data stays exportable either way.

Does Nomi record my conversations?

No. Nomi only listens when you tap the orb to leave yourself a note after a conversation, never during it, and it doesn’t keep recordings. It’s like texting yourself, but it understands what you said.

Where can I use it?

Right now in the web app, on any laptop or phone browser. The iPhone app is on its way.

Can I import the people I already know?

Yes. On iPhone you can pick people from your contacts; only the ones you choose are added.

How does the search work?

Ask a question in plain words. Nomi reads your own notes about each person and picks who fits by meaning, so “who could help with chemistry?” finds the chemical engineer even if you never wrote the word “chemistry”.

Who can see my notes?

Only you. Every account can only ever read its own data. We use an AI provider to process notes you send, only to give you the result.
