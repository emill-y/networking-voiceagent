Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Nomi exists to help you remember the people you meet, so it holds personal information about you and about them. We treat that as a responsibility. In short: you decide what goes in, it is only used to provide Nomi to you, we never sell it or use it for ads, and you can delete all of it at any time.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist we store your name, school and email, your personal invite code, and who invited you (if anyone). We use them only to let you in when your spot opens, to count the friends who joined with your link, and to send you a welcome email. To be removed, email us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address, and your name if you sign in with Apple or Google and choose to share it. Sign-in is handled by Supabase.
  • What you save. The people you confirm after a session: their names and details, what you talked about, and the session they came from (its name, date and length).

We don't collect your location, don't use advertising or tracking SDKs, and don't build profiles about you for anyone else.

Sessions

A session is something you start yourself, on your phone, in the web app or on your Apple Watch, for example when you walk into an event. Nomi only listens while a session you started is running, and always shows that it is listening. Before your first session Nomi asks you to confirm that you'll let people know you're taking notes: in some places, including California, everyone in a conversation must agree to be recorded.

While a session runs, Nomi records in pieces of about 20 seconds. Each piece is sent to our server, turned into text by our speech-recognition provider, and the audio is deleted as soon as the text comes back. Nomi never stores session audio.

The text is used only to find the people you were introduced to and what they said. Nothing is added to your network until you review the session. When you save or discard a session its transcript is deleted; a saved session keeps only its name, date, length and the people you chose to keep. A session left unfinished is deleted after 7 days.

How text is processed

To find people and their details, session text is sent to our AI provider (currently Groq, Inc.), and the result is shown to you to confirm. Searching in plain language sends your question and a summary of your notes the same way. We use the provider only to process your request, not to train models on your data.

Where it's stored

Your account and notes are stored with Supabase (hosted PostgreSQL in Canada), protected by row-level security so each account can only ever read its own data. Our website, web app and API are hosted on Vercel. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by these providers.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data, or share it for advertising.
  • Keep recordings of anyone's voice.
  • Listen when you haven't started a session.
  • Look people up or contact anyone on your behalf.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any person or session in the app. Removing a session keeps the people you saved from it; delete a person from their page.
  • Export everyone you've met as a spreadsheet: Profile → Export.
  • Delete everything or delete your account under Profile. This permanently removes your data.
  • Turn off microphone access any time in your phone's or browser's settings.

Children

Nomi isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact
