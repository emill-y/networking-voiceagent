Privacy policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Nomi exists to help you remember people, so it holds personal information about you and about the people you meet. We treat that as a responsibility. In short: you decide what goes in, it's only used to provide Nomi to you, we never sell it or use it for ads, and you can delete all of it at any time.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address, and your name if you sign in with Apple and choose to share it. Sign-in is handled by Supabase.
  • What you save. The notes you record or type about people you meet: their names and details, what you talked about, follow-ups, and the text of your notes.
  • Contacts you choose. If you import from your phone's contacts, only the people you select (name, company, job title, phone number and email) are sent to Nomi. Your address book is read and matched on your phone and is never uploaded as a whole.

We don't collect your location, don't use advertising or tracking SDKs, and don't build profiles about you for anyone else.

Your voice

Nomi only listens while you're recording, and shows that it's listening. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone using Apple's speech recognition. On devices that support it, this happens entirely on the phone; on older devices, Apple may process the audio under Apple's privacy policy. Nomi does not store your recordings.

In the rare cases where on-phone transcription isn't available, the recording is sent to our server, transcribed by our speech-recognition provider, and deleted as soon as the text is returned.

On the web

In the web app and in the live demo on our website, speech is turned into text by your browser's own speech recognition; depending on your browser this may happen on its maker's servers (for example Google for Chrome). Nomi receives only the resulting text. The demo works without an account: its text is sent to our AI provider to show you the result, and nothing from the demo is stored: the sample network lives only in your browser tab and disappears when you close it.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, Nomi asks Google for your email address and permission to view and edit events on your calendars. Nomi uses this only to show your upcoming events next to the people you might talk to there, and to add, update or remove the follow-ups you choose to date as events on your calendar. To suggest people for an event, its title and location are sent to our AI provider, the same way your searches are. Nomi does not store your calendar events, does not share or sell calendar data, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models. The access Google gives Nomi is stored encrypted, and you can disconnect anytime under You → Google Calendar or in your Google Account settings; disconnecting revokes Nomi's access.

Nomi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Events and "Plan Events"

To suggest real events, Nomi reads public event listings: the open tech-conference data published on GitHub, and any public calendar links (iCal feeds) you add yourself. Nomi's server fetches those links for you and keeps the links you added until you remove them or delete your data. To match events with your people, event titles and short descriptions and a summary of your notes are sent to our AI provider, the same way your searches are. The city and interests you enter are stored with your account. Nothing about your people is shared with event organizers; invites are only sent if you send them yourself.

Profile photo, sharing and invites

If you add a profile photo, a small copy is stored with your account and shown only to you. When you share contacts, Nomi stores a copy of their name, role, company, school, location, contact details, links and interests (never your private notes) for the email address you enter, until that person accepts or declines. If email sending is set up, Nomi emails that address once to tell them. Invite links carry a short code so we can count who joined through your invite.

Event providers

To find events near you, Plan Events sends the city you enter (or, if you choose "Near me", an approximate location), the dates and the categories you pick to event providers such as Ticketmaster and SeatGeek. Nothing about your contacts is sent to them.

How your notes are processed

To pull out names, details and follow-ups, the text of a note is sent to our AI provider (currently Groq, Inc.) and the structured result is shown to you to confirm. Nothing is saved until you confirm it. Searching your people in plain language sends the question the same way. We use the provider only to process your request, not to train models on your notes.

Where it's stored

Your account and memories are stored with Supabase (hosted PostgreSQL in Canada), protected by row-level security so each account can only ever read its own data. Our website, web app and API are hosted on Vercel. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by these providers.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data, or share it for advertising.
  • Look people up or enrich their profiles behind your back.
  • Contact anyone on your behalf. Call, Text and Email buttons open your phone's own apps.
  • Record in the background.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any person or moment in the app.
  • Export everything: You → Export my memories.
  • Delete your account: You → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and everything in it.
  • Turn off microphone, speech recognition or contacts access at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

Nomi isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
